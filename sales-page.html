FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Do I need GoHighLevel to use FilersHub?▼
FilersHub is built specifically for GoHighLevel users. The GHL integration is what makes it powerful — syncing contacts, automating workflows, and embedding right in your GHL dashboard. You'll need an active GHL account to get the full experience.
Will my clients see "FilersHub" anywhere?▼
No. The client portal is 100% white-labeled with your firm's logo, brand color, and custom URL. Your clients will think you built a custom app just for them.
What tax return types are supported?▼
We support 12 return types including Individual (1040), Joint, Separate, Head of Household, Amended (1040-X), Partnership (1065), S-Corp (1120-S), C-Corp (1120), Fiduciary (1041), Nonprofit (990), State Only, and Local returns.
How does the Chrome extension work?▼
Install it once from the Chrome Web Store. Click the icon to open FilersHub in a compact popup. When you're browsing a GHL location page, the extension automatically detects which location you're in and connects to the right firm — no manual switching needed.
Can I automate status-based workflows in GHL?▼
Absolutely. Every time a return status changes in FilersHub, a tag like fh-status:filed is added to the GHL contact. You can trigger any GHL workflow based on these tags — send emails, SMS, move pipeline stages, assign tasks, and more.
What happens to my price after launch?▼
Early access customers keep their rate for life. When we officially launch and raise prices, your subscription stays at the pre-sale rate as long as you remain active.
How many clients can I manage?▼
Unlimited. Both Core and Pro plans include unlimited clients. The only difference is the number of staff seats (team members who can log in and manage returns).
